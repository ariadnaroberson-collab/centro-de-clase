1. First-time setup

Do this once, on the classroom PC that's connected to the TV, in Microsoft Edge.

Why the same PC and browser matterEverything Herramientas remembers (your Canva links, points, stickers, rubrics, scores) is saved inside that browser on that computer. If you open Herramientas at home, or in Chrome instead of Edge, you'll see an empty dashboard. Nothing is lost; it's just on the other computer.
  1. Open Herramientas. Go to your Centro de Clase home page and click the 🧰 Herramientas tile, or go straight to ariadnaroberson-collab.github.io/centro-de-clase/herramientas.html. Bookmark it (Ctrl + D).
  2. Pick a class. Use the yellow Clase menu at the top. Every tool uses this class's student list, and points, stickers and scores are saved separately for each class.
  3. Choose your favorites. In the left menu, click the ☆ next to any tool you use every day. It turns gold ★ and appears under ⭐ Favoritas at the top of the menu. Click ★ again to remove it.
  4. Check your links. Open 🖥️ Mis enlaces. Cuentópolis, Los Guardianes de Chiquibul, Charlas de Calendario, Daily Agenda and The Passport Game are already saved. Add any others you use often.
  5. Allow pop-ups and the microphone. The first time you use the floating window, the score sheet window or Safari silencioso, Edge will ask for permission. Click Allow.
Your TV is a second screenDrag the Edge window with Herramientas onto the TV and press ⛶ for full screen. You can keep private things (the score sheet, results) on your own PC monitor.

2. Your daily routine

Before class (2 minutes)

  1. Pick the class in the Clase menu.
  2. Open 🖥️ Mis enlaces and paste today's Canva link in 🎨 Canva de hoy, then click 💾 Guardar y abrir. (Each class has its own; older links are kept under 🕘 Enlaces de días anteriores.)
  3. Optional: fill in 🎯 Qué hacer ahora for today's independent work.

During class

  • Teaching from slides: click 🔗 Enlaces ▾ → today's Canva. The small dock in the corner gives you a timer, Safari status, the Español meter and a name picker without leaving the slides.
  • Back to the tools: click 🧰 Herramientas (top-left of the slides or on the dock). Your slides stay open on the same slide; the gold 🖥️ button in the top bar takes you back to them.
  • Independent work: open 🐾 Safari silencioso and press ▶ Empezar. Animals come out while the class is quiet, even while your slides are showing.

After class

  • If you scored an oral or written check, open 📊 Resultados and click ⬇️ Descargar esta to save the spreadsheet.
  • About once a week, click 💾 Guardar copia at the bottom of the left menu (see section 11).

4. Showing Canva & Cuentópolis

Links open inside Herramientas, so your tools keep working behind them.

  1. Open a link. From 🔗 Enlaces ▾ in the top bar, or 🖥️ Abrir aquí in Mis enlaces or Práctica y juegos.
  2. Switch between open links. Each one gets a tab in the bar at the top (up to 5). Canva stays on the slide you were on.
  3. Go back to your tools. 🧰 Herramientas top-left. Return with the gold 🖥️ button.
  4. Close a link with ✕ Cerrar.

Making Canva show inside Herramientas

  1. In Canva, open your presentation and click Compartir (Share), top-right.
  2. Under Acceso por enlace (link access), choose Cualquier persona con el enlace → puede ver (anyone with the link can view).
  3. Click Copiar enlace and paste it into Mis enlaces. Herramientas converts it to a viewing link automatically, even if you copied the "edit" link.
If a page shows blank or an errorSome sites refuse to open inside another page (Google, sometimes Wayground, or Canva if your school blocks public links). Use ↗ Abrir en pestaña nueva to open it in a normal tab, and the 🪟 Flotante window to keep your timer and tools on top.

5. The dock & the floating window

The dock bottom-right, while a link is open

  • Timer ▶/⏸ starts and pauses Timer 1; the ⏱️ menu sets 1–15 minutes and starts it.
  • 🐾 3/8 Safari progress and noise bar. Tap to pause or continue Safari.
  • 🗣️ 4:12 Español minutes (only while that meter is on). Tap it when someone speaks English.
  • 🎵 ⏹ stops the music (only while music plays).
  • 🎲 Nombre picks a student and shows the name big.
  • 🧰 back to the tools · – shrink the dock · drag by ⠿ to move it.

🪟 Flotante Edge & Chrome only

A small window that floats on top of everything, even Canva in its own full-screen present mode or another program.

  • Timer with 1–10 minute buttons
  • Safari progress, noise level and ⏯
  • Español minutes with ⏯ and 🇬🇧 ¡Inglés!
  • 🎲 Nombre al azar and 🎵 ⏹

Drag it onto the TV and resize it from a corner. Close it with its own ✕. Keep the Herramientas tab open while you use it.

6. 🖥️ Presentar

🖥️ Mis enlaces Canva, Cuentópolis & links

  • 🎨 Canva de hoy One link per class, per day. Paste, name it (optional), 💾 Guardar y abrir. The last 8 are kept.
  • ⭐ Mis favoritos Links shared by all classes. Add with name + link → ＋ Añadir; remove with ✕.
  • 🖥️ Abrir aquí opens inside Herramientas · ↗ opens in a new tab.

✍️ Pizarra your board on the TV

  • Click the white board and type. A− A+ change size; the color dots change the text color.
  • 💬 Marco de oración adds a sentence frame ("___ quiere ___.").
  • 🔈 Leer reads the board aloud; 🐢 Lento reads it slowly.
  • ✏️ Dibujar lets you draw or circle on top (the color dots become pen colors). Click again to go back to typing. 🧽 Borrar dibujo / 🗑️ Borrar texto clear each layer.
  • The text is saved per class; drawings are kept until you close the page.

🎯 Qué hacer ahora work-time instructions

  • Shows the "Puedo…" objective, numbered steps and Cuando termines, with a timer beside them.
  • Tap a step to mark it ✅. ↺ Desmarcar pasos clears the checks.
  • ✏️ Editar → pick a template (Lectura en silencio, Trabajo en parejas, Diario, Estaciones, Actividad en la computadora) or write your own, one step per line, up to 6. Saved per class.

📱 Código QR

  • Pick a saved link (or paste any link) → 📱 Crear QR. Students scan it with their device.
  • ⛶ full screen · 🖨️ Imprimir a black-and-white sheet · 💾 Descargar imagen.

7. 📝 Evaluación y práctica

Keep grades off the TVStudents can see anything on the TV. Score students in the separate score-sheet window on your PC monitor, and only open 📊 Resultados on your own screen.

🎤 Evaluación oral y escrita proficiency checks

  1. Choose 🎤 Oral or ✍️ Escrita, the Nivel (Novato bajo → Intermedio), and a Rúbrica (or "solo nivel").
  2. Click ＋ Nueva evaluación and give it a name.
  3. Click 🪟 Abrir hoja en otra ventana and drag that window to your PC monitor. The TV only shows the question and whose turn it is.
  4. In the sheet: ▶ Siguiente estudiante picks someone who hasn't gone yet. Tap their level NL NM NH IL IM IH, score each rubric criterion 1–4 if you chose a rubric, and add a note if you like. Everything saves as you go.
  5. On the TV: 🎲 Otra pregunta for a new prompt; the ⏱️ buttons start a timer (30 s–2 min oral, 5–15 min written).

NL / NM / NH = Novato bajo / medio / alto · IL / IM / IH = Intermedio bajo / medio / alto.

📏 Rúbricas

  • Four example "Puedo…" rubrics: 🗣️ Hablar, ✍️ Escribir, 💬 Conversación, 📖 Leer y escuchar, each from 4 · ¡Lo logré! to 1 · Estoy empezando.
  • Show one with ⛶ Pantalla completa before a check so students know the target. 🖨️ Imprimir prints a black-and-white copy with a name line.
  • ✏️ Editar to type in your own (for example from your Master Assessment Folder Map). Add or remove rows; ＋ Nueva rúbrica for a new one.
School AI ruleType or paste your rubrics directly into Herramientas. They're saved only in your browser and never go through an AI tool.

🙋 Comprobación rápida quick check

  • Choose 👍 Pulgares, 🚦 Semáforo, ✊ Puño a cinco, ✅ Sí / No or 🔤 A B C D. Type your question.
  • Look at the hands and tap each card to count (−1 fixes a mistake). You'll see the bars, "Respuestas: 6 de 7" and a summary like "83% entiende".
  • Tick Esconder resultados mientras cuento to hide the numbers until you're done.

📋 Boleto de salida exit ticket

  • Type today's topic or word, then 📋 Nuevo boleto for a prompt that includes it. Tick Solo para pequeños for K-2-friendly prompts.

📊 Resultados

  • Choose an evaluation to see each student's level, rubric scores and notes, plus a bar summary of levels.
  • ⬇️ Descargar esta or ⬇️ Descargar todas saves a spreadsheet that opens in Excel, ready to copy into your gradebook or OneNote.
  • ✏️ Seguir evaluando reopens it; 🗑️ Borrar deletes it (can't be undone).

🎮 Práctica y juegos links by unit

  • Choose the Unidad. "🎮 Juegos de Centro de Clase" already has Descúbrelo, En Orden, Repaso de Dominio, Ronda Rápida, The Passport Game and the two oruga games.
  • ＋ Nueva unidad (for example "Unidad 3: Los animales"), then add its Quizlet, Wayground, Padlet or Canva links.
  • Click a card to open it inside Herramientas · ↗ new tab · 📱 QR code for students' devices.
  • The unit you last chose also appears in the top 🔗 Enlaces ▾ menu.

🦈 Ahorcado hangman with each unit's words

  • Open it from the menu or the 🦈 Ahorcado button in Práctica y juegos.
  • Nivel is set for each class automatically: Lower Elementary → Chica y sus amigos (fácil), Upper Elementary → Chica (más difícil), Middle School 1 → Los Vecinos, Middle School 3 → El Club del Barrio MS2, Secondary 1 and 2 → La Cuadrilla. Change it anytime; each class remembers its choice. El Club del Barrio MS1 is in the list too.
  • Choose a Unidad, or 🎲 Todas las unidades to mix them, then 🎲 Nueva palabra. Words don't repeat until the whole unit has been used.
  • Students guess by tapping the letters or typing on the keyboard. Accents don't matter: A also finds á. Middle School and Secondary also get short phrases ("el trabajo de verano").
  • 🦈 Tiburón (Elementary): every wrong letter brings the shark's fin closer and the water gets darker. Out of chances? The shark jumps out with "¡ÑAM!", but the swimmer is saved by the 🛟. ✏️ Clásico (Middle School and Secondary) draws the traditional figure. Switch styles and the number of Errores (5–10) anytime.
  • Clues: 💡 Pista (Spanish clue), 🖼️ Dibujo (emoji), 🇬🇧 Inglés (for you). At the end, 🔈 Escuchar says the word, and a win can add +1 💯 to Puntos de la clase.
  • ⭐ Mis palabras (at the bottom): add your own, one per line: palabra | pista | emoji | inglés. They appear as the unit "⭐ Mis palabras" for that level.

8. 🧑‍🏫 Manejo de clase

⏱️ Temporizadores up to 4 timers

  • Presets 0:30–15 min, ±10 s and ±1 min, ▶ start / ⏸ pause, ↺ reset. Click the name to rename it.
  • ＋ Añadir temporizador for up to 4 at once. They keep running when you change tools; an alarm sounds at 0.

🐾 Safari silencioso quiet-work game

  1. Pick a habitat: 🌳 Chiquibul (Chica y sus amigos), 🚀 El sistema solar (astronauts land on the Moon and Mars, plus a rocket, robot, satellite, alien, UFO, comet and shooting star), la selva de Belice, el arrecife de Belice, la sabana, el desierto, los polos, la granja or el bosque.
  2. Choose how many animals (5, 8 or all) and the Tiempo total for the work time.
  3. Press ▶ Empezar and allow the microphone. While it's quiet, the gold bar fills and the next animal comes out.
  4. When it gets loud, the animals shiver (a warning). If it stays loud, the newest animal hides again.
  5. Find them all and the class celebrates, with +5 (or your number) in 💯 Puntos de la clase.
  • Tap an animal to read its card ("Soy el jaguar…") with 🔈. The game pauses while the card is open.
  • Límite = how loud is "too loud" (move right if animals hide too easily). Sensibilidad = how much the microphone picks up.
  • No microphone? Untick Usar micrófono: animals come out with time, and you press 📢 ¡Ruido! or R.
  • ⛶ Pantalla grande fills the TV. 🌍 Abrir Shy Safari (original) opens the original website.
  • The microphone only measures volume. Nothing is recorded.

🎵 Sonidos y música

  • 🔊 Sonidos: campanita, ¡atención!, ding-dong, correcto, incorrecto, ¡ta-da!, aplausos, redoble, cuenta 3-2-1, timbre.
  • 🗣️ Frases: 12 phrases read aloud ("Uno, dos, tres, ¡ojos en mí!", "¡A limpiar!"…).
  • 🎶 Música de fondo: Música tranquila, Lluvia, Selva, ¡A limpiar!. Click again (or ⏹) to stop. It keeps playing when you change tools.
Music + SafariSafari hears the music as noise. Turn the volume down (or off) while Safari is listening.

🙋 Comprobación rápida

Now in Evaluación y práctica.

🎡 Rueda de nombres

Spin with 🎡 ¡Girar! or by clicking the wheel. Quitar al ganador removes each winner; ↺ Todos de nuevo puts everyone back.

🎲 Estudiante al azar

A quick random name (can repeat). For no repeats, use Llamada sin repetir.

✋ Llamada sin repetir

Nobody is called twice until everyone has had a turn. Remembered per class until you press ↺.

👥 Grupos al azar

Choose "Personas por grupo" or "Número de grupos", then 👥 Hacer grupos.

🃏 Parejas al azar

Random pairs; if the class is odd, the last pair becomes a trio.

🔀 Plan de asientos

Set the number of columns and 🔀 Mezclar. Click two seats to swap them. Saved per class.

⭐ Participación

Tap a student's card each time they participate. Red-bordered cards haven't participated today. Resets every day.

🎲 Dados y números

Roll 1–6 dice, or a random number in any range. It appears written in Spanish ("treinta y dos") with 🔈.

9. 🏆 Premios y conducta

🗣️ ¡Solo en español!

  • Press ▶ Empezar. The bar fills while the class speaks only Spanish.
  • When someone speaks English, press 🇬🇧 ¡Inglés! (or I, or tap 🗣️ on the dock). It takes away 1 minute, or resets to 0 if you choose.
  • Each Meta (3–20 min) adds points to 💯 Puntos de la clase. The class record is saved.
  • It keeps counting while you change tools or show slides.

💯 Puntos de la clase

The whole class works toward a goal and a prize you choose. +1, +5, +10 or −1. Safari and the Español meter add points here too.

🏠 Puntos por equipos

Teams (Jaguares, Tucanes, Monos Aulladores, Tortugas). Click a team's name to rename it. 🎲 Sortear miembros splits the class into the teams. Up to 8 teams.

🏅 Estudiante de la semana

Choose the student and the reason, then 📣 ¡Anunciar!. Don Pico announces it full screen with Chica and her friends. ⭐ marks students who have already won; the history is kept per class.

🕵️ Estudiante misterioso

🎲 Elegir en secreto at the start of class; 👀 Solo para el maestro shows you the name. At the end, 🎭 Revelar.

🌟 Tablero de stickers

Pick a sticker, then tap a student's card to give it. ↩︎ removes their last one.

🪙 Economía de fichas

Give tokens (+1, +3). In 🛍️ La tienda, choose the student and the prize to spend them. Edit the shop: one line per prize, name = price.

🎮 Sube de nivel

Every 10 XP is a new level: 🌱 Novato → 🧭 Explorador → 🎒 Aventurero → ✈️ Viajero → 🦸 Héroe → 👑 Leyenda → 🐉 Maestro Dragón.

🎁 Premio sorpresa

A prize wheel. Edit the list on the right (one prize per line) and click Guardar.

10. 🇪🇸 Herramientas CI / TPRS

🕺 TPR · Pausa activa

  • 🙋 Simón dice: students only move when they hear "Simón dice". 🗣️ Comandos: every command counts.
  • Choose ustedes (whole class) or tú (one student), a new command every 3–10 s, and 1–5 minutes. With 🔈 Voz on, commands are read aloud.
  • At the end: "¡Muy bien! Siéntense." ⏭ Un comando gives one at a time instead.

11. Saving, backups & class lists

What's saved, and where

  • Points, stickers, tokens, levels, seat plans, Canva links, favorites, rubrics, scores and settings save automatically, in Edge on the computer you're using.
  • Clearing Edge's browsing data (cookies / site data) erases them, so keep a backup.

Backups

  1. At the bottom of the left menu, click 💾 Guardar copia. A file like herramientas-copia-2026-11-3.json downloads.
  2. Save it in your school OneDrive. It contains student names and scores, so don't keep it on a personal drive.
  3. To restore (or move to a new computer), click 📂 Cargar copia and choose the file.

Changing the class lists

The student names come from the file data/clases.json in your centro-de-clase GitHub site. To change them, the easiest way is to ask Claude ("add Maria to Middle School 1"). It updates the file for you.

Renaming a classPoints and scores are saved by class name, so a renamed class starts fresh. Ask before renaming a class mid-year.

13. Troubleshooting

ProblemTry this
I don't see a new tool or changePress Ctrl + F5. Wait 5 minutes after an update and try again.
My points / links / scores are goneCheck you're in Edge on the classroom PC and the right Clase is selected. If the browser data was cleared, use 📂 Cargar copia.
Canva shows blank inside HerramientasSet the Canva link to "Cualquier persona con el enlace → puede ver" (section 4), or use ↗ + 🪟 Flotante.
Safari animals hide too easilyMove Límite to the right or Sensibilidad to the left. Turn background music down.
Safari never reacts to noiseAllow the microphone (click the 🔒 or 🎙️ icon in Edge's address bar → Allow) and check Usar micrófono is ticked.
The read-aloud voice sounds EnglishWindows needs a Spanish voice: Settings → Time & language → Speech → Add voices → Español (México). Then restart Edge.
The score sheet or floating window doesn't openEdge blocked a pop-up. Click the blocked pop-up icon at the right of the address bar → Always allow.
No sound at allClick anywhere on the page once (browsers need a click before playing sound), and check the PC and TV volume.
A student's name is missing or misspelledAsk Claude to update the class list (section 11).
