✏️ Quick Draw Dictation

12 words · draw fast · then guess!

How to play: Draw 12 boxes on the whiteboard (or use the printed sheet). Pick a round and press Start. You say the words — one right after the next. The player draws each word in the next box. Then the player guesses every drawing, you reveal the answers and score.

🙈 The class can't see the words on this screen. The TV only shows box numbers.
To read the words: and drag it to your computer screen (it follows the game and has Start / Pause / Stop).
